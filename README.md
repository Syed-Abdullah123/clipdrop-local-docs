# Clipdrop Local

> Turn your phone into a local file-sharing server and transfer files instantly between mobile and desktop devices on the same network.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8e1742d6-51cc-4de6-a239-fa86930f2a3b" alt="ClipDrop Local Banner" width="100%">
</p>

**ClipDrop Local** is a React Native application that lets you share files between your phone and other devices using a web browser. Your phone acts as a local server, so you don't need to upload your files to the cloud or create an account.

Simply open the app, start the server, and connect your browser using the QR code or IP address shown on your phone.

You can then send and receive files, images, videos, links, and text between your devices.

## How to Use

### 1. Connect Your Devices

Open ClipDrop Local and make sure your phone and the device you want to connect are on the same Wi-Fi network.

Once the server starts, the app will display an IP address and a QR code that you can use to connect your browser.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6c764a31-1514-4ac3-b785-01dba7fc4fe8" alt="ClipDrop Local connection screen" width="100%">
</p>

You can connect in either of these two ways:

- **Scan the QR code:** Scan it with your other device to open the web interface in your browser.

- **Enter the IP address:** Open a browser on your other device and enter the IP address displayed in the app.

Once your browser connects successfully, the connection status will change from ```No browser connected``` to something like ```1 browser connected```.

**Note:** Both devices need to be connected to the same local network for the connection to work.

### 2. Open the Web Interface

After entering the IP address or scanning the QR code, you'll see the ClipDrop Local web interface in your browser.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/051ac37e-2ae7-43ad-857d-31742ec9214d" alt="ClipDrop Local web interface" width="100%">
</p>

You can use this interface to view shared content and send files directly from your computer to your phone.

There's no need to install anything on your computer. A compatible web browser is all you need.

### 3. Share Different Types of Content

ClipDrop Local supports more than just file transfers. You can send text, links, images, videos, audio files, and other documents between your connected devices.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/863ebc46-046f-46c9-ae20-aaf7f73d82bd" alt="Different types of content supported by ClipDrop Local" width="100%">
</p>

Whether you want to copy a link from your phone, send an image to your computer, or transfer a document, you can do it through the app or the browser interface.

### 4. Send and Receive Files

File sharing works in both directions: from your phone to your browser and from your browser to your phone.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0c8af325-0299-437b-b74d-a3ca2a411c3c" alt="Sending and receiving files with ClipDrop Local" width="100%">
</p>

Choose the content you want to share and send it to the other device. Your shared items are displayed in the interface, making it easy to see what's been transferred.

### 5. Play Videos in the App

ClipDrop Local also includes a built-in video player, so you can play supported video files directly in the app.

<p align="center">

This makes it convenient to access and play video files without needing to switch to another app.

## Features

- **Local file sharing** — Transfer content between your phone and a web browser on the same network.

- **No accounts required** — Open the app and start sharing without signing up.

- **QR code connection** — Connect your browser without manually typing the IP address.

- **Two-way transfers** — Send files from your phone to your computer and back.

- **Multiple content types** — Share text, links, images, videos, audio files, and documents.

- **Built-in video player** — Play supported video files directly in the app.

- **Browser-based access** — No dedicated desktop application is required.

## A Few Things to Know

- Your phone and the connected device need to be on the same local network.

- Keep the app running while you're using its local server.

- Large file transfers may not always complete successfully yet, and this is an area I'm still working on improving.
